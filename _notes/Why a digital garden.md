- I've been journalling (consistently) since I was 8 years old! I will write about this someday and how that practice has shaped me. My journals are all private. Despite this I actually dislike my writing style! It's not something I'm proud of and I've hesitated sharing my thoughts and ideas publicly (e.g. on Substack, or Twitter) because of that. This is cozier, between private journaling and public posts on social media.
- It's fun to curate ideas.
- Vanity, tbh
- I love the idea of my grandkids reading through these one day when I'm gone
- Wanting to be known and to be seen by more people. If some of my ideas resonate with you, talk to me!
