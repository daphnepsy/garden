- The Hong Kong Basic Law acts as Hong Kong's "mini-constitution," functioning as the region's primary governing document. Hong Kong's high degree of autonomy is not inherent; it is completely delegated and authorized by the Central People’s Government via national legislation
- This is analogous to the NY City Charter and the NY State Constitution. NYC's autonomy is not inherent, and is delegated by the state of NY.
