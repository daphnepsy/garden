### Questions we will discuss as a group:
- Are US / China actually in an AI race? Are they at risk of actually entering an AI race?
- Under what conditions is verifiable US–China AI coordination feasible? Is it feasible?
- Who are the main actors / resources? What are the different goals of each actor / government? How do they think about AI?

### Format
We will meet every other week for ~2 hours at [Collider NYC](https://collider.nyc/). You don't need a China or policy background!

## Session 1
*Grounding and framing the discussion around US/China AI Relations*

#### Reading
- Required: AI2040
- Optional
  - AI2027 
  - Selective Optimism: a critique of AI 2040 - by Richard Ngo 
  - IIId. The Free World Must Prevail - SITUATIONAL AWARENESS 
  - “The arms race model and its alternatives” Section of this blog post
 
#### Discussion questions
- Are the US / China actually in an AI race? Are they at risk of actually entering an AI race?
- What do we want to focus on?
- Who are the main actors / resources? What are the different goals of each actor/govt? How do they think about AI?
- How feasible is cooperation between the U.S. and China in AI development? Unlikely? Possible? Inevitable?

#### Takeaways
- Understanding what’s going on in China right now (related to AI development and strategy)
- Understanding how US-China relations will evolve in the medium term
- What is your ideal outcome from the U.S. - China AI “race”
- What can we as people do to impact outcomes?
