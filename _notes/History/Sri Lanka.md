## History
- Many ancient kingdoms (Kandy being the last one)
- First Portuguese, then Dutch, then English
- British colony from 1814-1948 (British Ceylon)
    
## Ancient Kingdoms of Sri Lanka
- Anuradhapura Period (500 BC - 1000 AD)
    - Established by North Indian migration
    - Sinhala script, which evolved from the Indian Brahmi script
    - Considerable literature in old Sinhalese grew around the Pali Canon
    - Trade with Rome around 0 AD
    - Ports all over country
    - Chinese as well
    - Pearls and gem stones and spices
    - Buddhism introduced in Sri Lanka in 3rd century BC
- Polonnaruva period
    - 1055-1235 AD
    - Capital moved south
- Transitional period
    - 1232-1594 AD
    - Power struggles between several kingdoms
    - Involvement of European powers and Chinese and Malaysians in Sri Lanka’s politics
    - Portuguese arrived in 1505
- Kandy
    - Got wrecked by the Brits (as we all were)
