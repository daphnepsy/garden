(in all lower case, in honor of [[love ethic]] og bell hooks)

I've long struggled to reconcile my love for robots with my desire to be a fully virtuous human. What does it mean to be fully virtuous? I subscribe to the [[love ethic]] from bell hooks.

Robotics is about optimization, and optimization + capitalism (in this day and age) is incoherent with the love ethic. I don't feel like capital-T Tech treats those who are being displaced with enough care and dignity. Would like to explore the topic a bit more.
