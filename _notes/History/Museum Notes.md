## National Museum of Anthropology (CDMX)
- First humans crossed through from Mongolia via the Bering Strait 40,000 years ago
- Preclassic period
    - 2300BC - Spanish conquerors in the 16th Century
    - The preclassic period, development of mesoamerican cultures
    - Teotihuacan, Toltec, Mexica
- Teotihuacan
    - Center of civilization for a while
    - Controlled obsidian mining, centralized production of obsidian goods in the city
    - Founded around 1stC, Collapsed in ~ 750AD
    - Pyramid of feathered serpent, moon, sun
    - Had a Citadel
    - Human sacrifice was common
- Toltecs
    - Skilled warriors
    - Based out of Tula
    - Influenced Mayan and Aztec cultures
    - Made cool sculptures
- Mexica/Aztecs
    - Moved from Aztlan, migrated for 200 years.
    - Claimed to descend from Toltecs
    - Settled in Tenochtitlan, founded in 1325AD
    - Q: how did the Mexica tell time?        
    - City fell to Spaniards in 1521

## British museum (London)
### Greece
- The period of [Greek history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Greece) from about 1600 BC to about 1100 BC is called [Mycenaean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mycenaean_Greece) in reference to Mycenae
    - This is where the culture was first recognized
- Minoans were not Greek, though the Mycaenean did dominate them eventually around 1450 BC
- Minoan -> Crete, Helladic -> Mainland Greece, Mycaenean -> Late Helladic
- Bronze Age of Greece (3200-1100BC)
- King Minos is mythical
- Minoans were probably powerful, as indicated by the myth of the Minotaur (Athenians were sent as sacrifice, in the Labyrinth of Knossos)

### Egypt
- The ancient Egyptian civilization lasted for over 3000 years (2686 BC - 395 AD)
- 31 dynasties in total
- First dynasty began with unification of upper and lower Egypt
- Old, Middle, and New Kingdoms represent periods of stability
- Intermediate periods represent eras of instability
- Persians ousted the last true pharaoh in 343BC
- Alexander the Great ended Persian occupation.
- Rome annexed Egypt in 30BC
- End of Ancient Egypt marked by split of Roman Empire

### Mesopotamia
- “Land between the rivers” (Tigris and Euphrates)
- Includes modern Iraq, northeast Syria, Southeast Turkey
- Between 6000BC and 1500BC, developed first civilisations and empires
- First cities were in Mesopotamia (eg Erbil, in modern Iraq)
    - Established around 3500-3000BC
    - They had irrigation!
- Akkadian empire
- Sumerians
    - Writing began in ancient Iraq (3300BC)
        - Sumerian script
        - Used for book keeping
        - Pictographs originally evolved into cuneiform script
- Babylonians
    - Rose to prominence after the Third Dynasty of Ur collapsed in 2000BC; Mesopotamia split into a number of small city-states
    - King Hammurabi (1792-1750 BC)
