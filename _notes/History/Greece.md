## Agora and Acropolis museum
- Stoa for shopping center
    - A social magnet
    - Mix of Doric and ionic columns
- Agora was the center of civic life, people like Socrates hung out there
- Similar to modern town centres, Roman forums etc
- Many objects from the classical and early Hellenistic periods re law, politics, weighing standards
- Acropolis was burned in 450BC ish by Persians during the Persian Wars
    - Athens was mostly abandoned by then
    - Parthenon was built after the Persians, remained untouched for many centuries
- Olive tree and other tree near the Erectheion mark the site where Athena and Poseidon met
- Panathenaic games, held yearly, included a parade through the city to the top of the Acropolis, and included a sacrifice to Athena
- Everyone is salty about the Elgin Marbles
    - Elgin marbles were taken by the Earl Elgin during Ottoman occupation of Greece
- Our first hotel (Herodon) is on one of the oldest streets in Athens, has held the same name since 1000 BC

## Greco-Buddhist Art
- [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greco-Buddhist_art)
- Coolest genre of art ever
- Believed to have produced the first representations of Gautama Buddha (THE Buddha) in human form
- Buddhism was never big in Greco-Roman Mediterranean. First contact was through Alexander the Great, but back then Buddhism wasn't even a thing in India yet. Only when Mauryan Emperor Ashoka converted + spread it in the region.
