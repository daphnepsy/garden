## History

### Roman Empire
- Roman Empire was preceded by the Roman Republic (ended w Julius Caesar), which was preceded by the Roman Kingdom
- Split into Eastern and Western because the empire got too big and became difficult to manage
- Famous emperors include Augustus, Justinian, Hadrian

### Western Roman Empire
- Hun drove Goths, who previously lived in steppes (in Ukraine, Russia) westward into Roman territory
    - Mass immigration of Goths
    - Alaric (Goth) decided that Western empire would be better for his people
    - 70% of Empire tax take were used to support Empire’s army
    - Eventually led to fall of western empire

### Eastern Roman Empire
- Modern day Istanbul (“in the”, “city”) was previously known as Constantinople (“Constantine” + “city”, polis) and before that, Byzantium
- Theodosian walls were three layers, and were supposed to be able to hold off attackers
    - But the ottomans used gunpowder and cannons!! This marked a new beginning in military history
- Istanbul straddles Asia and Europe, separated by the Bosphorus

### Ottoman
- Greece won its war of independence against the Ottomans in 1824
    - 9 stripes on the flag mark a quotation / slogan that was anti-Ottoman
    - Cross represents Christian history
    - Colours unknown
- How did the Ottomans take over?
    - They claimed whole eastern empire after Constantinople fell in 1400s (on May 29!)

Naples
- Comes from Greek (nea (new) + polis (city)
    - Was an Ancient Greek colony, one of many in Italy
- Subterranean Naples
    - Initially dug out by Greek slaves to obtain building material
    - Later used as a cistern, but use of cistern was abolished after cholera (or something like that) went around
    - Later used as WW2 bunkers, some rly stinky toilets
    - After WW2, some people remained here because their houses were destroyed
    - Some people literally live above an old Roman theatre
