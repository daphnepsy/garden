## Geography
- Anatolia refers to the Asian part of Turkey
- Turkey bordered by Syria, Iraq, Iran, Armenia, Georgia on the Asian side
- Turkey bordered by Greece and Bulgaria on the European side

## History
- Byzantine period, followed by Ottoman Empire, followed by Republic
- Emperor Justinian I and empress Theodora: Built Hagia Sophia (v3), survived Nika Riots by rounding up rebels in the hippodrome and executing them
- Mehmed II: conqueror of Constantinople, using cannons to bomb the Byzantine walls, prev thought to be impregnable
- Mehmed V was the last sultan, not Abdulhamid II
- Suleiman the magnificent
    - 10th Ottoman sultan
- Mirmar Sinan the Architect
- Osman was the OG (Ottoman means Osmanli)
- 12 wars were fought between Russians and Ottoman Empire between 16th and 20th C

## Hagia Sophia
- Built 1500 years ago
- Literally “holy wisdom” in Greek
- Constructed three times, last time by emperor Justinian
- Destroyed twice by riots / protests
- After Ottoman conquest, converted into a mosque

## Topkapi palace
- Islamic calligraphy: 6 scripts
- 4 caliphs
- Mohammed from 7th Century (died in ~636AD)
- Keep his beard in boxes
- Very cool collection of guns, horse face shields, swords etc

## Dolmabache Palace
- Cost 100% of tax revenue at time of construction (5 million durums)
- Largest crystal chandelier in the world in the ceremonial room, possibly the best room I’ve ever seen
- Contains artefacts from daily life of sultan and family
- Many cool paintings to demonstrate might of Ottoman army and navy

## Museum of Turkish and Islamic Art
- Long history of various Islamic empires
- Many of these were bloody
- First 4 caliphs (after Mohammed) made up the first caliphate, and rapidly expanded Islam in ~30 years
- Some of the descendants of Genghis led Islamic empires too
- Rugs were made with natural dyes
- A lot of artifacts are in the Netherlands for some reason

## Blue Mosque
- Built with 6 minarets, but due to clash with the number of minarets of the mosque at Mecca (Mecca needs to have more minarets), a 7th minaret was added to the one at Mecca
- Cats inside!
- Prayer 5 times a day, but doesn’t need to be at a mosque (except on Friday, for prayer); Friday is also a typical day off as a result
- Coffee was discovered in Ethiopia, after a goat herder found that his goats were more energetic after trying coffee berries
- Abbas ibn Firnas was the first man to try “flying”. Jumped off a minaret, somehow didn’t die (but did sustain serious injuries on the second attempt)
