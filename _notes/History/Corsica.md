## History
- 1453-1755: Republic of Genoa
- 1755-1769: Corsican Republic
- 1769-now: French territory

## Republic of Genoa
- Genoa’s origins as a significant power trace to the early medieval period, emerging from the wreckage of the Western Roman Empire and subsequent Lombard and Frankish rule of northern Italy.
- By the 10th century it was functioning as a self-governing commune — one of the first in Europe — run by local merchant families rather than a feudal lord. The republic was formally consolidated around 1099, roughly coinciding with the First Crusade, which is not a coincidence.
- Directly sat North of Corsica on map!
- Rivaled the republic of Venice (which if you remember, took Crete!)
- Christopher Columbus was Genoese
- Italy as a unified nation didn’t exist until 1861. Between collapse of Western Roman Empire (~476, when the last emperor was deposed) and 1861, Italy was made of bunch of competing city-states, kingdoms, and foreign-controlled territories.
- Florence, Venice, Genoa, Milan, Naples, the Papal States all developed distinct identities and competed intensely
